AFFIX MERCHANT
Buy. Craft. Corrupt. Profit.

A pocket-sized ARPG crafting shop. Buy cheap weapons at the market, reroll and forge their affixes like in Path of Exile, then sell your masterpieces to picky adventurers for a fortune. One bad click can shatter everything.

FEATURES
- Deep affix crafting: add, reroll, swap, excise, lock and forge prefixes and suffixes (tiers T5 to T1)
- Item stability: every craft costs stability. Push your luck or sell while you can
- Void Corruption: one final gamble that boosts, shifts or destroys your item
- 8 characterful buyers with their own tastes, bounties and reactions
- Hot market trends, daily goals, a 70-entry affix Codex with permanent rewards
- Prestige: sell your shop for Master Points and permanent upgrades
- Collectible-card art style, all drawn in code, with holographic foil on rare items
- Dark-fantasy sound design. Share your best weapon as an image card
- Built for phones: one screen, no scrolling. Also plays on desktop
- English and Slovak. Saves automatically in your browser

FREE vs PREMIUM ($3)
The free version contains the whole game: all gameplay, all crafting, prestige and 5 weapon skins.
Premium ($3) supports development and adds:
- Ambient forge soundtrack (crackling fire and dark drone)
- 3 exclusive animated weapon skins: Infernal, Frostcrown and Royal

Your progress is stored locally in your browser.
